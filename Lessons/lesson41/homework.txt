11. Amaliy mashqlar
Quyidagi 15 ta masala hayotiy vaziyatlarga asoslangan. Har birida qaysi JOIN turi kerakligini avval o'zingiz aniqlashga harakat qiling, so'ngra PostgreSQL so'rovini yozing.

1. Do'kondagi barcha mahsulotlar nomini va ular tegishli kategoriya nomini bitta ro'yxatda chiqaring.  [oson]

SELECT
    p.name as product_name,
    c.name as category_name
FROM products p
JOIN category c
    ON p.category_id = c.id;

2. Barcha xodimlar ismini va ular ishlaydigan bo'lim nomini chiqaring.  [oson]

SELECT
    x.name as xodim_name,
    b.name as bo;im_name
FROM xodim x
JOIN bolim b
    ON x.bolim_id = b.id;

3. Kutubxonadagi barcha kitoblar va ularni yozib olgan mualliflar ismini chiqaring.  [oson]

SELECT
    b.name as book_name,
    a.name as author_name
FROM book b
JOIN author a
    ON b.author_id = a.id;

4. Hali birorta bo'limga biriktirilmagan xodimlarni ham ro'yxatdan tushirib qoldirmasdan, barcha xodim–bo'lim juftliklarini chiqaring.  [o'rta]

SELECT
    x.name as xodim_name
    b.name as bolim_name
FROM xodim x
LEFT JOIN bolim b 
    ON x.bolim_id = bolim.id;

5. Hozircha hech qanday xodimi yo'q bo'limlarni ham ko'rsatadigan tarzda, har bir bo'lim va unda ishlaydigan xodimlar sonini hisoblang.  [o'rta]

SELECT
    b.name as bolim,
    count(x.id) as xodim_count
FROM bolim b 
LEFT JOIN xodim x 
    ON b.id = xodim.bolim_id
GROUP BY b.id, b.name
ORDER BY b.name;

6. Onlayn-do'konda buyurtma bermagan mijozlarni toping.  [o'rta]

SELECT 
    m.name as mijoz_buyurtmasiz
FROM mijoz m 
LEFT JOIN buyurtma b 
    ON m.id = b.mijoz_id
WHERE b.id is NULL;

7. Hech kim buyurtma qilmagan mahsulotlarni aniqlang.  [o'rta]

SELECT
    b.name as buyurtma
FROM buyurtma b
LEFT JOIN mijoz m 
    ON b.id = m.buyurtma_id
WHERE m.id is NULL;

8. Har bir xodim ismi qatorida uning bevosita rahbari ismini ham ko'rsating.  [o'rta]

SELECT 
    x.name as xodim_name,
    r.name as rahbar_name
FROM xodim x 
LEFT JOIN rahbar r 
    ON x.rahbar_id = r.id;

9. Kompaniyadagi ikkala jadvaldan ham (xodimlar va bo'limlar) hech kimni va hech qanday bo'limni tashlab ketmasdan, to'liq ro'yxat (mos kelmagan joylarda bo'sh) tuzing.  [qiyin]

SELECT 
    x.name as xodimlar,
    b.name as bolimlar
FROM xodim x 
FULL JOIN bolim b 
    ON x.bolim_id = b.id;

10. Restoran menyusidagi har bir taom va u tayyorlanadigan barcha ingredientlarni ko'rsatuvchi hisobot tuzing.  [o'rta]

SELECT
    d.name AS dish_name,
    i.name AS ingredient_name
FROM dishes d
INNER JOIN dish_ingredients di
    ON d.id = di.dish_id
INNER JOIN ingredients i
    ON di.ingredient_id = i.id
ORDER BY d.name;

11. Sport turnirida har bir jamoa boshqa har bir jamoa bilan bir marta o'ynaydigan barcha mumkin bo'lgan o'yinlar jadvalini (juftliklarni) hosil qiling.  [qiyin]

SELECT
    x.name as home,
    y.name as away
FROM teams x
CROSS JOIN teams y
WHERE x.id < y.id;

12. Bir xil bo'limda ishlaydigan xodimlarni, o'zini o'zi bilan juftlashtirmasdan va har bir juftlikni faqat bir marta chiqaradigan tarzda, ikkitadan guruhlab chiqaring.  [qiyin]

SELECT
    e1.name AS employee_1,
    e2.name AS employee_2,
    e1.department_id
FROM employees e1
INNER JOIN employees e2
    ON e1.department_id = e2.department_id
    AND e1.id < e2.id;

13. Kiyim do'konida mavjud barcha o'lchamlar (S, M, L, XL) va barcha ranglarning mumkin bo'lgan kombinatsiyalarini hosil qilib, ombor uchun to'liq variantlar ro'yxatini tuzing.  [oson]

SELECT 
    s.name as size,
    c.name as color
FROM size s 
CROSS JOIN color c 
ORDER BY s.name, c.name;

14. Mijozlar, ularning buyurtmalari va buyurtma qilingan mahsulotlar — uchta jadvalni birlashtirib, hech qachon buyurtma bermagan mijozlarni ham ro'yxatdan chiqarib tashlamasdan umumiy hisobot tuzing.  [qiyin]

SELECT
    c.name AS customer_name,
    o.id AS order_id,
    p.name AS product_name
FROM customers c
LEFT JOIN orders o
    ON c.id = o.customer_id
LEFT JOIN order_items oi
    ON o.id = oi.order_id
LEFT JOIN products p
    ON oi.product_id = p.id
ORDER BY c.name;

15. Universitetdagi barcha talabalar va ular tanlagan fanlar ro'yxatini, hali birorta fan tanlamagan talabalarni ham ko'rsatadigan tarzda chiqaring, so'ngra fan tanlamaganlar sonini alohida hisoblang.  [qiyin]

SELECT
    s.name AS student_name,
    c.name AS course_name
FROM students s
LEFT JOIN student_courses sc
    ON s.id = sc.student_id
LEFT JOIN courses c
    ON sc.course_id = c.id
ORDER BY s.name;


SELECT COUNT(*) AS students_without_course
FROM students s
LEFT JOIN student_courses sc
    ON s.id = sc.student_id
WHERE sc.student_id IS NULL;
